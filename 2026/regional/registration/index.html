ICPC Jakarta Registration

Last modified: 28 Sep 2026, 01:26 WIB

All Indonesian teams must follow selection rules described in Eligibility Rules page, meanwhile all foreign teams from the Asia Pacific and other super-regions may register directly to The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest.

Coaches are responsible for completing the team registration via ICPC global.

  • Go to https://icpc.global/private/teamRegistration.
  • Select “Asia Pacific Contests” as the region.
  • Select “The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Onsite Regional Contest”. Please log in or sign up to access the registration site.
  • Select your team origin institution. If your institution is not listed, click “Not in the list? Suggest New Institution” and submit your institution.
  • Enter your team name and team members. If a team member cannot be found in the system, click “Register new person” to register the team member.
  • If you are registering more than one team, click the “+” button and repeat the previous step.
  • Review and confirm all team registrations by clicking “Register Team(s)”.
  • After submitting the registration, the eligibility of all team members will be reviewed by the ICPC Committee.
  • Please wait until all team members have been confirmed as eligible before making any payment.
  • Once all team members have been confirmed as eligible, the coach will receive the official payment link by email from the ICPC Committee.
  • Check the coach’s registered email address for the payment link. Please also check the spam/junk folder if the email is not found in the inbox.
  • Complete the registration fee payment using the official payment link provided by email.
  • Your registration and payment process is complete once the payment has been successfully submitted.
IMPORTANT: Do not transfer the ICPC registration fee directly to the Bina Nusantara University bank account. The ICPC registration fee must be paid using the official payment link sent by email after all team members have been confirmed as eligible.
