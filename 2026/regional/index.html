Prizes

The following prizes are given for The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest:

  • Merch for the first solvers of each problem.
  • Prizes for the highest ranked national teams.
  • Prizes for the highest ranked teams from the highest ranked institutions. The rank of institutions are the ranks of the highest ranked team of each institution.
  • Medals (3 Golds, 4 Silvers, 5 Bronzes) for the highest ranked teams.

Important Dates

  • Registration: 28 August 2026 to 10 November 2026
  • Practice Session: Saturday, 28 November 2026
  • Contest Day: Sunday, 29 November 2026 from from 10:00 to 15:00 (GMT+07)
