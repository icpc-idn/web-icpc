Indonesia National Contest (INC) 2026

Indonesia National Contest (INC) 2026 is a national programming contest that serves as the online preliminary round for Indonesian teams to advance to The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest. Teams will be challenged with a set of 10 to 13 problems to be solved in 5 hours.
