INC Registration

Last modified: 28 Sep 2026, 01:26 WIB

Coaches are responsible for completing the team registration.

  • Go to https://icpc.global/private/teamRegistration.
  • Select “Asia Pacific Contests” as the region.
  • Select “The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Onsite Regional Contest”, then select “The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta – Indonesia National Contest”, and choose “Indonesia National On-Line Contest”. Please log in or sign up to access the registration site.
  • Select your team origin institution. If your institution is not listed, click “Not in the list? Suggest New Institution” and submit your institution.
  • Enter your team name and team members. If a team member cannot be found in the system, click “Register new person” to register the team member.
  • If you are registering more than one team, click the “+” button and repeat the previous step.
  • Review and confirm all team registrations by clicking “Register Team(s)”.
  • Your team registration is complete once the registration has been successfully submitted.

Registration Fee

  • Registration Fee: IDR 150,000 per team (exclusive of bank/transfer fees).
  • Payment Deadline: Monday, 12 October 2026

The registration fee must be transferred to the following Bina Nusantara University account:

  • Bank Name: Bank Central Asia (BCA) KCP Bina Nusantara, Jakarta
  • Account Number: 527-1706-678
  • Account Name: UNIV BINA NUSANTARA
Important: Please make sure that the payment has been transferred to the correct Bina Nusantara University account and that all information submitted in the form is accurate.

After completing the payment, the coach or any team member must submit the payment confirmation through the official INC 2026 Payment Confirmation Form: https://tr.ee/INC2026PaymentConfirmationForm

Payment confirmation must be submitted no later than Monday, 12 October 2026.

After submitting the form, you will receive an automatic acknowledgment email confirming that your payment submission has been received.

Please note that the automatic email does not confirm that your payment has been successfully verified. The INC 2026 Committee will review your payment, and you will receive a separate confirmation email once the payment has been successfully verified.
