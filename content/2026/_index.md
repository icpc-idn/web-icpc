International Collegiate Programming Contest is an annual multi-tiered computer
programming competition among the universities of the world.

BINUS UNIVERSITY ([binus.ac.id](https://binus.ac.id/)), which has moved toward
a world-class university, will host one of the Asia Regional Contest.
This year is the eighteenth time BINUS hosts this most prestigious programming
contest in Asia Pacific region.
Hopefully, by holding this contest, it would give more chances to Indonesian
college students to take part and get more experiences.
