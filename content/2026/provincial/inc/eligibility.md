Indonesia National Contest (INC) 2026 follows eligibility rules from ICPC World Finals and ICPC Asia Pacific Championships:

  * General eligibility rules: https://icpc.global/regionals/rules
  * Asia Pacific eligibility rules: https://icpc.jp/apac/2026-27/rules/

There is no specific eligibility rules for INC. In general, these are the team requirements:

  * Each team must consist of exactly 3 students and 1 coach from the same institution.
  * All team members must be eligible to participate in the ICPC Asia Pacific Championship 2027.
  * Each student may compete in only one team in the Indonesia National Contest 2026 and The 2026 ICPC Asia Regional Jakarta.
  * A coach is allowed to coach more than one team.
  * The coach must be an official staff or faculty member of the university.
