### Prizes

The following prizes are given for The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest:

  * Merch for the first solvers of each problem.
  * Prizes for the highest ranked national teams.
  * Prizes for the highest ranked teams from the highest ranked institutions. The rank of
institutions are the ranks of the highest ranked team of each institution.
  * Medals (3 Golds, 4 Silvers, 5 Bronzes) for the highest ranked teams.
