The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest follows eligibility rules from ICPC World Finals and ICPC Asia Pacific Championships:

  * General eligibility rules: https://icpc.global/regionals/rules
  * Asia Pacific eligibility rules: https://icpc.jp/apac/2026-27/rules/


# Local Teams

For local Indonesian teams, the following rules will be applied to select **55 local teams** for The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest:

  * Top 5 teams of the Compfest 18 Provincial Contest.
  * Top 40 teams of the INC General group.
  * Top 5 teams of the INC Supplementary group.
  * Up to 5 golden tickets from INC General groups participants.

Golden Tickets are given to top teams who solve at least 1 problem in INC 2025 and their institution is not in Top 40 INC 2025 and Top 5 in COMPFEST 18 Provincial Contest.
Only at most one Golden Ticket will be given to each institution, and only the team with the highest rank in INC 2026 from each institution qualifies for the Golden Ticket.

If the number of local teams does not reach 55 teams, the next best teams from the INC General group will be invited to The 2026 ICPC Asia Jakarta Regional Contest.

There is no additional registration fee for local teams.
